Validation: static checks pass across 41 HTML pages. Browser checks cover contact placement, consent/conversion behavior, keyboard focus and first-visit language suggestions (28 passing scenarios). Desktop and mobile screenshots were reviewed. Repeated generation is stable for all four homepages. Chrome/Chromium was used; physical mobile devices and Safari were not tested. The design detector reported advisory findings only.

The owner authorized publication of the complete update on 22 October 2026. Deployment and production verification are recorded separately in `outputs/home-privacy-publication.json`.
