## Billing and scope

- Travel within Warsaw plus the first hour: from 250 PLN.
- Each additional hour: from 150 PLN.
- A short nearby job, agreed beforehand and successfully completed: from
  200 PLN, as an alternative to the standard visit.
- Larger installations may use a project quote instead of hourly billing.
  The same labour is never charged under both methods.
- Materials, equipment, extra work and partial-hour billing are agreed before
  work. No invented device prices, flat job fees or rounding rules.

Six home services have no attached fees in the catalogue. Sidebars use the
same three visit/hour rates. Business cards, subscriptions, SLA and office
examples are replaced with home situations and a plain referral to the
corresponding ITBIZ services section.

## Search and technical changes

and JSON-LD share one source. Unconditional Mesh superiority and two-hour
response claims are removed. Runtime intent variants retain the existing
five allowlisted keys and conversion labels while describing homes.

Canonicals, reciprocal hreflang, form IDs, API delivery and analytics settings
are preserved. The original pre-CRO fixture is unchanged; reviewed hashes
identify these SEO changes. PHP redirects now go directly to the established
canonical directory URLs. Desktop service navigation avoids redirect hops.

The new stylesheet uses existing brand tokens/fonts, a linked scope list and
a separate billing panel. Shared JS/CSS references use `20261009-home1` to
avoid stale cached intent text. Legal/tool edits cover shared asset references
and common business schema only.

schema now matches those hours; visits remain by agreement. Inaccurate relative
