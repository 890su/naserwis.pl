# NaSerwis.pl — current product definition

**Updated:** 2026-10-26

## Purpose

NaSerwis is the main public and organic-search site for practical on-site Wi-Fi,
LAN, RJ45 and CCTV work in Warsaw. It accepts both private customers and
organisations under the actual scope and pricing agreed before work.

## Audience

- private customers, apartments, houses and home offices;
- small firms, offices and organisations needing a concrete one-off task;
- PL/RU/UK/EN speakers in the real service area.

An organisation does not have to move to ITBIZ to submit a request.

## Service architecture

- Keep one canonical page per real service.
- Use examples or short subsections to explain differences between a home and an
  organisation when the work is essentially the same.
- Keep genuinely home-specific guides and examples; they do not make the whole
  brand home-only.
- Do not create mirrored `dla domu` / `dla firm` pages solely to repeat the same
  offer or capture the same query.
- Owner request, 2026-10-26: business service destinations now live locally in
  `/dla-firm/` with five concise task pages in all four locales. These describe
  office access, work interruption, authorisation and agreed deliverables; they
  use the existing progressive form with page-specific request examples.
- ITBIZ may be linked once, contextually, for recurring support or a materially
  distinct business infrastructure project. The link is voluntary.

## Commercial rules

- Short nearby job from 200 PLN, by agreement and when completed successfully.
- Standard Warsaw visit with first hour from 250 PLN.
- Each additional hour from 150 PLN.
- Agree scope, timing and expected price before work.
- Do not promise unconditional arrival times, fixes, compatibility or results.

## Experience and design

- Preserve the current approved design, forms, reviews, contact paths and URL
  structure unless a concrete task changes them.
- The first screen should communicate technical diagnosis and hands-on service,
  not generic household imagery.
- Keep equivalent quality and intent across PL/RU/UK/EN.
- Preserve canonical/hreflang, schema, consent and conversion contracts.

## SEO role

NaSerwis owns broad organic demand for Wi-Fi, LAN, network faults, cabling/RJ45
and CCTV service. Content should be useful and specific, not lengthened merely
for word count. The homepage introduces the offer; service pages answer their
own intent; guides support the relevant service.

## Evidence rules

Existing public reviews and verified service facts may be used. Do not invent
clients, cases, certifications, response times, ratings or documentary photos.
Treat dated audits and release notes as history, not current positioning.

## Change authority

This definition guides future work but does not authorize deployment, ad-account
changes or indexation changes. The workspace-level `PROMOTION-CONTRACT.md` is the
current cross-brand source of truth.
