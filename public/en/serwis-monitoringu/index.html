What is wrong with your CCTV?

One camera, the whole system or just the app? That is where we start. Choose a symptom to see what we check.

No picture or interference from a camera

A black screen, moving lines or a “no signal” message. We check power and the signal path. For analogue systems, this includes the cable, BNC connectors and compatibility with the DVR input.

IP camera offline or disconnecting

The picture drops out and returns. For IP systems, we check the cable, port and PoE power; for wireless cameras, we also check Wi-Fi coverage and network settings.

Recorder not recording or beeping

Live view works, but recordings are missing. We check drive status, schedules, date and recording settings. We read audible warnings alongside the device’s error message.

Cannot view cameras on a phone

The monitor shows a picture, but the app does not. We check internet access, settings after a router change, and account permissions in the manufacturer’s app.

Blurry image, especially at night

We check the lens and cover, focus, lighting and infrared reflections. We establish whether settings, camera position or a component need changing.

False alarms or missing notifications

We check detection zones, sensitivity, schedules and notifications. Available settings depend on the camera and recorder model.

If you need the recordings, do not format the drive or factory-reset the system before diagnosis. Tell us when you get in touch.

Camera installation and system expansion

A new system or an extra camera? First, we agree what you need to see and where recordings will be stored.

  1. Agree the scope

    Camera numbers and positions, daytime and night lighting, cable routes and how long recordings need to be kept.

  2. Install and configure

    Agreed cabling, camera connections, power and recorder setup. For an extension, we check compatibility with your existing equipment.

  3. Test the result

    We check the picture, recording and playback, plus agreed phone access. We show you how to use the system.

Which systems do we work with?

  • IP and Wi-Fi. Network cameras, NVR recorders, and PoE or wireless connections.
  • Analogue. CVBS, AHD, HD-TVI and HD-CVI: video signals, cabling, BNC connectors and power.
  • Hybrid. Analogue and IP cameras in one system, where the recorder supports the required formats and channel modes.

We confirm repair or expansion options after checking the models and installation. Not every DVR supports every format and resolution. Circuit-board repairs and data recovery from damaged drives are outside this service.

What should you tell us?

Your location, number of cameras, recorder model and the symptom. Tell us whether local viewing, recording and phone access work, and what changed before the fault. Do not send passwords or private footage through the form.

Questions before a visit

Can you service a system fitted by another installer?

Yes, after checking the equipment, installation and available management permissions. Tell us the device models. Replacing the whole system is not always necessary.

Can I add a camera to my existing recorder?

We first check spare channels, device compatibility, power, network capacity and recording storage. Then we agree the extension.

Does losing phone access mean recording has stopped?

Not necessarily. Local recording may still work when remote access fails. We check these functions separately during diagnosis.

What affects installation cost?

Camera numbers, cable routes and condition, mounting positions, recording setup and access settings. Equipment, materials and larger jobs are quoted separately before work starts.
