How we restore a working connection

We test the line and ports before proposing a repair. We test the connection again when the work is complete.

  1. 01
    Measure

    We check the cable, socket, switch port and connection parameters.

  2. 02
    Locate

    We identify the break, loose termination or failed component.

  3. 03
    Repair

    We replace the connector, socket, cable section or port configuration.

  4. 04
    Final test

    We confirm stability and achieved speed before completing the job.

Questions before a visit

Must the entire cable be replaced?

Not always. We check the cable and its ends first. Repair options depend on the damage and access to the installation.

Do you repair home fibre or camera connections?

We agree these tasks individually after a description and inspection. We work within the agreed home installation scope; provider-side faults are referred to the provider.
