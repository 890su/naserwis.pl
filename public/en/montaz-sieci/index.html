Installation FAQ

At what stage of construction should the network be installed?

Ideally before plastering. This way all cables can be hidden in walls. If plastering is already done, we use aesthetic cable trunking or baseboard channels.

What cables do you use?

The standard is copper twisted pair (not aluminum!) Cat. 6, providing throughput up to 10 Gbps.

How much does wiring an office with 20 workstations cost?

The cost depends on cable run lengths and installation method (in-wall, cable trunking). A typical office project for 20 workstations is 3,000–6,000 PLN for labour plus materials. We prepare a preliminary estimate after a call and agree the visit fee in advance.

Do you install network cabling in residential homes?

Yes, it's one of our most common services. We install LAN sockets in every room, connecting TVs, gaming consoles and computers to a stable wired connection.

How long does a LAN installation take?

A 3–4 room apartment typically takes 1 working day. A larger house or office — 2–3 days. We provide exact timelines after an on-site inspection.

Do you provide as-built documentation?

Yes, upon request we prepare a network diagram with cable route descriptions and port labeling. For businesses — this is standard with every project.
