You pay for the agreed scope of work. We do not charge both an hourly rate and a separate service fee for the same work.

The 200 PLN starting rate applies to a short job nearby when the task is successfully completed. It is an alternative to a standard visit, not an additional charge.

Larger installations can be quoted as a project instead of hourly billing. Materials, equipment, extra work and billing for part of an hour are agreed before work starts.

Starting prices depend on job conditions and are not an offer. We confirm the amount before work starts. No VAT is charged.
