WiFi FAQ

Why is my internet slow when I'm paying for a fast connection?

Most often the problem is poor router placement, interference from neighbors, or outdated equipment. Optimizing settings or a Mesh system can significantly improve the situation.

Is a Mesh system better than a signal booster?

Definitely yes. Boosters create separate networks and cut bandwidth in half. Mesh provides a single seamless connection with automatic switching between points.

Why is my WiFi slow?

The most common causes are: router placed too far or behind a wall, WiFi channel congested by neighbors, outdated router supporting only 2.4 GHz. We diagnose the problem on-site and recommend the optimal solution.

Do you fix WiFi in offices?

Yes, we serve both homes and offices. For businesses we offer professional access points, VLANs and managed hotspots — with documentation and warranty.

How much does WiFi diagnostics cost?

WiFi diagnostics starts from 150 PLN and includes signal analysis, router settings check and recommendations. We often resolve the issue during the first visit.

Can I extend WiFi range without cables?

Yes, Mesh systems connect wirelessly and don't require cabling. Simply plug additional units into power outlets in the right locations.
